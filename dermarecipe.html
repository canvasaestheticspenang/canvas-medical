What is inside the serum

Two lead ingredients, supported by four familiar skincare actives.

NMN

Nicotinamide mononucleotide at 10,000 ppm, the lead ingredient of the range.

PDRN 4-Complex

Four DNA-derived ingredients: histidine, arginine, magnesium and sodium DNA.

Niacinamide 2%

Supports a brighter, more even-looking complexion.

Panthenol

Supports a comfortable, hydrated skin feel.

Adenosine

A functional ingredient widely used in Korean anti-wrinkle cosmetics.

Hyaluronic acid

Five molecular sizes for layered hydration.

How to use

  1. Cleanse and prep. Cleanse, then apply toner or essence as usual.
  2. Serum. Apply RejuNMN Repair Serum to the face and neck.
  3. Cream. Finish with RejuNMN Repair Cream. Press gently with your palms to help it absorb. Use morning and evening.
