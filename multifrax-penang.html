What is MultiFrax?

MultiFrax is a dual-wavelength fractional laser that combines 1550 nm and 1927 nm in a single handpiece. Its scanning technology can fire the two wavelengths individually or simultaneously, so texture, pigmentation, scarring and sun damage can be addressed together in one pass. The 1550 nm wavelength works deeper in the dermis to stimulate new collagen and elastin, while the 1927 nm resurfaces the epidermis to refresh pigment and surface texture.

At CANVAS Medical Clinic in George Town, Penang, MultiFrax is selected and supervised by our physicians. As a non-ablative fractional laser it renews the skin through microscopic treatment zones rather than removing the whole surface — meaningful resurfacing with a recovery most people manage in a few days.

1550 nm + 1927 nm Fractional resurfacing Acne scars & texture Pigment & sun damage Collagen remodelling ~3–5 days recovery

How it works

  1. Consultation & skin assessment. A CANVAS physician examines your skin, reviews your history and confirms MultiFrax suits your concern and skin tone.
  2. Wavelength & settings selection. Your physician chooses 1550 nm, 1927 nm or both, and the depth and density, to target texture, scarring, pigment or a combination.
  3. Treatment. After numbing cream, the handpiece scans the area with fractional pulses — a warm sensation with a light snap. A full face is usually completed in well under an hour.
  4. Aftercare & series. Expect some redness or light flaking for a few days. We guide sun protection and recovery, and plan a series for gradual, natural resurfacing.

Who it's for

MultiFrax suits a range of skin-quality concerns, but suitability is always confirmed at consultation. You may be a good candidate if you want to:

  • Soften the look of acne scars and uneven texture
  • Refine enlarged pores and fine lines
  • Even out pigmentation and sun damage
  • Improve overall skin quality and radiance
  • Remodel collagen for firmer-looking skin
  • Resurface with a short, manageable recovery

MultiFrax is not suitable during pregnancy, over active infection or breakouts in the area, or with certain photosensitising medications and conditions. Deeper scarring may need a combined plan. Your physician will confirm suitability and expectations at consultation.

MultiFrax vs other lasers

MultiFrax is a fractional resurfacing laser that remodels texture, scars, pigment and sun damage across two wavelengths in one pass. PicoSure Pro is a picosecond laser that shatters pigment with a photoacoustic effect and has less downtime — ideal for pigmentation and tattoos. Dermablate is an ablative resurfacing laser for deeper texture goals with more recovery.

Fractional resurfacing for texture, scars and quality: MultiFrax. Pigment and tone with minimal downtime: PicoSure Pro. Deeper ablative resurfacing: Dermablate. Many plans combine them, and your CANVAS physician will sequence what your skin needs.
