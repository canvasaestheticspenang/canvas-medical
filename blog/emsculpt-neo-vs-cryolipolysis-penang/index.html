EMSCULPT NEO vs Cryolipolysis:
Choosing a Body Treatment

Both are non-surgical body contouring treatments. Both are backed by clinical evidence. But they take completely different approaches to changing your shape, and picking the wrong one for your goals means a disappointing result.

The short answer

EMSCULPT NEO builds muscle and reduces fat at the same time, using a combination of heat (radiofrequency) and muscle contractions (HIFEM energy). Cryolipolysis reduces fat only, by freezing and permanently destroying fat cells in a targeted area. If your goal includes muscle tone, EMSCULPT NEO does something cryolipolysis simply cannot.

What is EMSCULPT NEO?

EMSCULPT NEO combines radiofrequency heating with High-Intensity Focused Electromagnetic (HIFEM) energy in a single session. The radiofrequency element warms the treatment area and can reduce subcutaneous fat, while the HIFEM element induces powerful, involuntary muscle contractions — the equivalent of thousands of maximal contractions in a session no gym workout could replicate.

It's commonly used on the abdomen, buttocks, thighs and arms, for patients who want to build muscle definition alongside fat reduction.

Muscle building Fat reduction HIFEM energy Radiofrequency heat No downtime

What is Cryolipolysis?

Cryolipolysis uses controlled cooling to target and crystallise fat cells in a specific area, without harming the skin or surrounding tissue. The body then naturally clears the damaged fat cells over the following weeks, permanently reducing the fat layer in that zone.

It does not build muscle or affect tone — it addresses stubborn fat pockets that resist diet and exercise, such as the flanks, abdomen or under the chin.

How to decide

EMSCULPT NEO may suit you if you:

  • Already exercise regularly and want to enhance muscle definition
  • Want both fat reduction and toning in one programme
  • Are targeting the abdomen, buttocks or thighs for a more sculpted look

Cryolipolysis may suit you if you:

  • Have a specific, stubborn fat pocket that hasn't responded to diet or exercise
  • Are not seeking a muscle-toning result, just fat reduction
  • Prefer fewer sessions spread further apart

Some patients combine both — cryolipolysis to reduce a stubborn fat pocket, followed by EMSCULPT NEO to build tone in the same or adjacent area.
