Beautiqlo Lab at CANVAS

A focused Korean skincare line: a collagen hydrogel mask, an NAD+ cream and sunscreen, and a noni foam cleanser.

Book a Consultation View the Range

About the brand

Beautiqlo Lab is a Korean skincare label with a short, practical range. Each product below is shown with its key ingredients and how to use it.

Derma Matrix Collagen Lift Mask

A hydrogel sheet made from concentrated ampoule, with 300 Da low-molecular collagen. The sheet turns clear as it is absorbed.

Key ingredient
300 Da low-molecular collagen
Format
Two-piece hydrogel sheet for the upper and lower face
How to use
Cleanse and tone. Apply the lower piece from the mouth area, then the upper piece around the eyes. Leave on until the sheet turns clear, then remove and finish with cream.
Also
Can be used as an overnight mask. Cleanse and continue your routine in the morning.

NAD+ Cell Energy Cream

A rich repair cream with an NAD+ formula for dry, tired-looking skin. Creamy texture that absorbs without stickiness.

Key ingredients
NAD+ formula, ceramide NP, panthenol, Centella asiatica extract
Texture
Creamy, non-sticky finish
Testing
Dermatologist tested, as stated by the brand
How to use
Last step of your routine. Spread over face and neck, then pat gently to absorb.

NAD+ UV Defence Sun Screen

Daily sun protection from the NAD+ line.

Protection
SPF 50+ PA++++
Use
Morning, as the final step before make-up. Reapply through the day.

Noni Deep Cleansing Foam

A foaming facial cleanser with noni.

Size
150 ml tube
Use
Lather with water, massage over damp skin, rinse.
