What is Nanoneedling?

Nanoneedling uses a device fitted with ultra-fine nano-tips to create microscopic channels limited to the skin's outermost layer. Unlike microneedling, which reaches into the dermis to trigger deeper collagen renewal, nanoneedling works entirely at the surface — refining texture and dramatically improving how well active serums are absorbed, without ever piercing the skin.

At CANVAS Medical Clinic in George Town, Penang, nanoneedling is offered as a gentle, physician-guided treatment on its own or paired with our medical facials, and makes an easy lunchtime addition to a skin-maintenance routine.

How it works

  1. Consultation. A CANVAS clinician assesses your skin and confirms nanoneedling suits your goals and skin type.
  2. Preparation. The skin is cleansed; no numbing cream is needed since the treatment does not pierce the skin.
  3. Treatment. The nano-tip device is glided across the skin, often alongside a tailored serum for enhanced infusion.
  4. Finish & glow. Skin looks refreshed immediately with no visible downtime; a course is planned for progressive results.

Who it's for

Nanoneedling suits most skin types and concerns, always confirmed at consultation. You may be a good candidate if you want to:

  • Try a gentler entry point into skin needling before considering microneedling
  • Boost the results of your skincare or in-clinic serums
  • Get an immediate, no-downtime glow before an event
  • Care for sensitive or reactive skin that isn't ready for deeper needling
  • Fit an effective treatment into a lunch break
