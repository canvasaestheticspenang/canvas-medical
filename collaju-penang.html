What is Collaju?

Collaju is an injectable collagen preparation from Regenbogen, supplied as a 3% collagen syringe and described by its manufacturer as a tissue supplement of collagen. Collagen is the protein that gives skin its thickness, firmness and resistance to creasing, and it is the thing the skin steadily loses with age, sun exposure and time. Collaju addresses that loss the direct way: by placing collagen into the dermis rather than adding a gel that holds water, or waiting months for the skin to build its own.

At CANVAS Medical Clinic in George Town, Penang, Collaju sits in the collagen group of our injectable formulary alongside Linerase and Deusaderm. It is a skin-quality treatment, not a volumiser — the right choice when skin has grown thin, crepey or slack rather than hollow, and the wrong one when what you actually want is contour.

How it works

  1. Consultation and assessment. A physician examines your skin, takes a full history including allergies, and confirms whether a collagen injectable is the right tool for what you want changed.
  2. Mapping the areas. Treatment zones are planned to where the skin has thinned or creased, rather than applied uniformly across the face.
  3. Injection. The collagen preparation is placed into the dermis with a fine needle or cannula, with topical anaesthetic where appropriate.
  4. Course and maintenance. A short initial course spaced a few weeks apart, then periodic maintenance — with review so the plan follows your response rather than a template.

Who it's for

Collaju may suit you if you:

  • Have skin that has become thin, crepey or slack rather than hollow
  • Want firmness and skin quality rather than added volume or projection
  • Prefer a result that improves the skin instead of reshaping the face
  • Are willing to complete a short course and maintain it periodically
  • Want an honest assessment of whether collagen or another injectable fits better
  • Understand results build with the course rather than appearing at once

Collaju is not appropriate during pregnancy or breastfeeding, with active infection or inflammation in the treatment area, or where there is a relevant allergy or sensitivity. Because it is collagen-based, your physician will take a careful history before proceeding. Results differ between individuals, and suitability is always confirmed at consultation.

Collaju vs the other collagen injectables

Collaju is a 3% collagen preparation for skin structure and firmness. Linerase is heterologous type I collagen used for dermal restructuring and elasticity, often on skin that has thinned noticeably. Deusaderm is the formulary's other collagen-based injectable, supporting structure and resilience.

Where the goal is hydration and bounce rather than structure, Profhilo or a skinbooster may fit better, and where it is genuinely volume, that is a dermal filler conversation instead. Our physicians will tell you which of those you are actually having.
