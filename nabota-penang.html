What is Nabota?

Nabota is a botulinum toxin type A developed in Korea and used internationally — marketed as Jeuveau (sometimes called "Newtox") in the United States. It is a well-established, purified neurotoxin that temporarily relaxes the muscles responsible for dynamic wrinkles, softening the overlying skin.

At CANVAS Medical Clinic in George Town, Penang, every Nabota treatment is planned and administered by an LCP-certified physician. Nabota is often chosen for dependable, natural-looking results and good value — and we dose it precisely to your anatomy so you leave looking refreshed, not treated.

Also known as Jeuveau Frown Lines (11s) Forehead Lines Crow's Feet Brow Lifting Dependable value

How it works

  1. Consultation & assessment. Your physician examines your facial muscles at rest and in motion, discusses your goals, and advises whether Nabota suits your anatomy.
  2. Marking & dosing. Injection points are mapped to your individual anatomy and dosing is planned precisely. We avoid over-treatment as a principle.
  3. Injection. Very fine needles deliver small amounts of Nabota to the targeted muscles. Most patients describe minimal discomfort; the procedure takes 15–20 minutes.
  4. Review at 2 weeks. Results develop over three to seven days and are fully visible at two weeks. We offer a review to assess the outcome and make any minor adjustments if needed.

Who it's for

Nabota may be appropriate if you:

  • Have frown lines, forehead lines or crow's feet that appear with expression
  • Want dependable, natural-looking results at good value
  • Want a non-surgical brow lift or softening of a heavy brow
  • Are looking to slim or soften a square jaw from masseter hypertrophy
  • Are beginning a preventative programme before lines become static
  • Are in good general health with realistic expectations

Nabota is not suitable during pregnancy or breastfeeding, or with certain neuromuscular conditions. All contraindications are assessed at consultation.
