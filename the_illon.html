The iLLON at CANVAS

Korean cosmeceutical skincare focused on absorption, from daily cleansers and toners to professional treatment kits. The range is organised into the brand’s four lines: Primary, Enhance, Core and Unbody.

Book a Consultation View the Range

About the range

The iLLON is a Korean brand built around one idea: skincare works better when it is absorbed well. Its daily products are formulated to be gentle and slightly acidic, and its professional line pairs active ampoules with delivery methods such as micro-needle kits, spicule peels and treatment devices.

Our team can advise which products suit your skin, and whether a professional treatment such as our iLLON facial fits your goals. Tap any product photo to see the full gallery.
